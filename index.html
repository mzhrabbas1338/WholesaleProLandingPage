📌 System Overview & Architecture

WholesalePro Billing & Management System is engineered for wholesale businesses, traders, and bulk stock distributors requiring high transaction velocity, precision inventory tracking, and offline reliability.

Key Capabilities include SQLite local database storage, automatic WhatsApp bill transmission, Samar Traders A4/POS receipt formatting, and mobile B2B ordering portal integration.

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts & Excel-Like Navigation

• Ctrl + S: Save & Print Current Invoice

• F2: Quick Item Search & Add to Bill

• F9: Open Customer Ledger Summary

• Enter / Tab: Move seamlessly across item table cells without mouse interaction.

🤖 WhatsApp Bot & Anti-Ban Queue Engine

• Automated Balance Enquiry: When a customer sends .balance, the background listener responds with their current outstanding ledger balance.

• PDF Ledger Request: Texting .ledger generates and transmits a complete statement PDF.

• Anti-Ban Protection: Mass broadcasts are queued with 8-15 second random delays to protect numbers from WhatsApp enforcement filters.

📦 Fiscal Year Closing Procedure

1. Navigate to Settings -> Backups & Sync -> Fiscal Year Closing.

2. Enter period label (e.g., 2025-2026) and click Close Current Period.

3. The system generates a standalone SQLite archive (`database_25-26.sqlite`), resets transaction history, and carries over final balances as new Opening Balances.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Does WholesalePro work offline without internet? +
Yes! WholesalePro is built offline-first using local SQLite database technology. You can issue invoices and track stock even if your internet connection is down. Internet is only required when syncing WhatsApp messages or Cloud B2B orders.
How does the WhatsApp Anti-Ban protection work? +
Our broadcast queue uses humanized random delays (8 to 15 seconds per message) and local Link Identity (LID) resolution to ensure your WhatsApp number remains safe and compliant.
Can retailers order directly from mobile phones? +
Yes! WholesalePro comes with a responsive B2B Mobile Ordering web portal. Retailers log in with their customer code, browse stock rates, and place orders directly to your desktop cashier queue.
